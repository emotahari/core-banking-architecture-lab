# Day 04 Exit Ticket — Ownership

- Timebox: 5 minutes
- Lesson and references: closed

## Questions

1. Distinguish Trigger Owner, Decision Authority and State Owner for a deposit credit requested by Lending.
2. Why do a Reference, Snapshot or Projection not become the Authority merely because they store data?
3. Can Lending Principal Outstanding and Accounting Receivable Balance have different owners without violating single authority? Explain their semantic difference.
4. What metadata must make an executed-agreement Snapshot trustworthy?
5. What may a cross-domain Process Manager own, and what must it not own?

- authority distinctions: 2
- copy semantics: 1
- balance/process reasoning: 2

Pass: 4/5. Shared authority for one semantic fact is a critical miss.
