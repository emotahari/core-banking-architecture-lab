# Day 01 Exit Ticket — Domain Strategy

- Timebox: 5 minutes
- Lesson and references: closed
- Answer in Week 02 Workbook

## Questions

1. In one sentence distinguish a Subdomain from an Application.
2. Why is `Core Subdomain` not synonymous with every critical banking function?
3. Can the same Subdomain be Core for one bank and Supporting for another? Give one banking example and the strategic force.
4. A team says «Accounting is complex and high-risk, therefore it is Core.» What is missing from the argument?
5. State one concrete consequence of classifying a Subdomain as Core and one risk of falsely classifying too many Subdomains as Core.

## Rubric — 5 points

- correct distinctions: 2
- strategy/evidence reasoning: 2
- investment consequence: 1
